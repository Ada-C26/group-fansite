## Accessibility Needs

We will communicate openly if either of us needs extra time, clarification, or support. If one of us has a question or problem, we will try to solve it together.

## Collaboration vs. Individual Work Expectations

We have decided to divide the project into four independent sections. Rayanna will be responsible for the index page, Valerie for the gallery, Tatsiana for the facts page, and Hardeep for the activities page. To maximize everyone’s learning, each team member will complete all three waves for their assigned section.

Although we have divided the responsibilities, we will support one another whenever questions or challenges arise.

Each team member will work on a separate branch. We will review changes together before merging them into the main branch to help prevent merge conflicts and maintain consistency across the project.

## Learning Style

We prefer to work independently on our assigned sections and then come together to review our progress, share feedback, and address any challenges.

We will meet during class on Tuesday, October 27, and Wednesday, October 28, as well as during class breaks if needed, to coordinate our work and ensure the project is completed.

## Preferred Feedback Style

We are open to feedback. At the end of each Wave, we will review the work together and discuss anything that needs to be changed before submitting or moving forward.

## One Team Communication Skill to Improve

We want to improve our communication by keeping each other updated about our progress and clearly explaining any changes we make to the project.

## Optional: Other agreements

We will use slack as our main communication tool.

We will keep a written record of our progress in our slack group or conversation, including updates about changes made to the project.

We will practice presenting and explaining our code together so that both partners understand the complete project and are prepared for the presentation.
