About the Series

Harry Potter is a seven-book series by J.K. Rowling, published between 1997 and 2007. It follows Harry from his eleventh birthday, when he learns he is a wizard, through his seven years at Hogwarts and his fight against the dark wizard Voldemort. The books became eight films, a stage play, and a theme park.

Why We Love It

  • The characters: Harry, Ron, and Hermione feel like real friends by book two.
  • The magic: spells, potions, and creatures that make the world feel alive.
  • Hogwarts: a castle with moving staircases and a hundred secrets to find.

The Four Houses

  • Gryffindor: brave and daring. Harry, Ron, and Hermione's house.
  • Hufflepuff: loyal and hardworking. Home of Cedric Diggory.
  • Ravenclaw: clever and curious. Luna Lovegood's house.
  • Slytherin: ambitious and cunning. Produced Snape and Draco Malfoy.
