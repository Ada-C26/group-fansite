Behind the Books

  • Rowling came up with the idea in 1990 on a delayed train from Manchester to London King’s Cross.
  • Philosopher’s Stone was rejected by 12 publishers. Bloomsbury took it after the chairman’s 8-year-old daughter read it and loved it.
  • In the US it was retitled Sorcerer’s Stone.
  • Harry shares Rowling’s birthday: July 31.
  • Order of the Phoenix is the longest book in the series.
  • The books have been translated into 80+ languages, including Latin and Ancient Greek.

Names & Wordplay

  • “Tom Marvolo Riddle” is an anagram of “I am Lord Voldemort.”
  • “Dumbledore” is an old English word for bumblebee.
  • Hermione’s name pronunciation is spelled out in Goblet of Fire when Krum struggles with it.

Behind the Films

  • Alan Rickman was told key secrets about Snape’s backstory long before the final book came out.
  • Hagrid’s big scenes used a body double, former rugby player Martin Bayfield.
  • Deathly Hallows was split into two films; the series ended in 2011.

Magic in the Real World

  • King’s Cross Station has a Platform 9¾ sign with a luggage trolley disappearing into the wall.
  • Quidditch became a real sport, renamed quadball in 2022.
  • Hermione’s Patronus is an otter, Rowling’s favorite animal.
