Fun Facts About Halloween

1. Halloween History

Halloween is celebrated on October 31. It started from an old festival called Samhain. People celebrated the end of summer and the start of winter.

2. Pumpkin Carving

People make funny or scary faces on pumpkins. In Ireland, people first used turnips. In America, they started using pumpkins because they are bigger and easier to carve.

3. Halloween Costumes

Many people wear costumes on Halloween. They dress up as witches, ghosts, vampires, superheroes, or their favorite movie characters.

4. Black Cats

Black cats are a symbol of Halloween. Some old stories say that black cats are connected to witches and magic. Today, many people simply think they are cute pets.

5. Trick-or-Treating

Children wear costumes and visit houses to get candy. They knock on doors and say, "Trick or treat!" Many families give them sweets and chocolate.

6. Halloween Colors

Orange and black are popular Halloween colors. Orange is the color of pumpkins and autumn leaves. Black is often linked to night and spooky stories.

7. Scary Decorations

People decorate their homes for Halloween. They use fake spiders, skeletons, ghosts, and lights. Some people make their houses look like haunted places.

8. Halloween Parties

Many people have Halloween parties. They wear costumes, play games, eat snacks, and watch scary movies. Some parties also have costume contests.

9. Bats and Spiders

Bats and spiders often appear in Halloween decorations. Bats come out at night, and spiders make webs. These animals help create a spooky feeling.

10. Halloween Candy

Candy is a big part of Halloween. Children often collect chocolate, lollipops, and other sweets. Some people also give small toys or stickers.

Have a fun and happy Halloween!
