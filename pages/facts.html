Halloween Fun Facts

Halloween is full of interesting traditions, spooky stories, and fun surprises! Here are five facts you might not know.

1. Halloween is over 2,000 years old

Halloween has roots in the ancient Celtic festival of Samhain, which marked the end of the harvest season.

2. Pumpkins were not always used

People in Ireland originally carved turnips to make jack-o'-lanterns before pumpkins became popular in America.

3. Trick-or-treating has a long history

Modern trick-or-treating developed from older traditions of visiting houses and receiving food or treats.

4. Black cats are Halloween symbols

Black cats became associated with Halloween because of old superstitions about witches and magic.

5. Halloween is celebrated on October 31

Halloween takes place every year on October 31, the evening before All Saints' Day.
