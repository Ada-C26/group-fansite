Halloween History

Halloween started from an old festival called Samhain. People celebrated the end of summer and the beginning of winter.

Pumpkin Carving

People in Ireland first carved turnips for Halloween. Later, people in America started using pumpkins because they were easier to carve.

Halloween Costumes

People wear costumes on Halloween. They dress up as witches, ghosts, monsters, and their favorite characters.

Black Cats

Black cats are a popular symbol of Halloween. Long ago, some people believed that black cats were connected to witches and magic.

Trick-or-Treating

Children dress up and visit houses to collect candy. This tradition became popular in the United States during the 1900s.

Happy Halloween!
