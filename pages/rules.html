Badminton Rules: Know the Game!

New to badminton? Whether you're picking up a racket for the first time or brushing up on the basics, this guide covers the rules that keep the game exciting and fair.

1. Objective of the Game

The goal of badminton is to hit the shuttlecock over the net and into your opponent's court. You want to make it difficult for your opponent to return the shuttlecock before it touches the ground.

2. Scoring

Badminton uses rally scoring, which means a point is awarded after every rally, no matter who served. A game is played to 21 points. You must usually win by 2 points, but if the score reaches 29-29, the next point wins the game.

3. Serving Rules

A serve starts each rally and must travel diagonally into the opponent's service court. If the server's score is even, they serve from the right service court. If the score is odd, they serve from the left.

The server and receiver must stand in their service courts without touching the boundary lines, and the serve must be delivered legally.

4. Faults: What Is Not Allowed?

A fault ends the rally and awards a point to the opponent. Common faults include hitting the shuttlecock outside the court boundaries, failing to hit it over the net, hitting the shuttlecock twice in succession, or touching the net with your racket or body while the shuttlecock is in play.

5. Singles vs. Doubles

Singles matches have one player on each side of the net, while doubles matches have two players on each side. Singles uses a narrower court during rallies, while doubles uses the full width. The service boundaries also differ between singles and doubles.

6. Winning a Match

Official badminton matches are generally played as the best of three games. The first player or team to win two games wins the match.
