Pumpkin Spice Latte

A pumpkin spice latte is a popular autumn drink combining coffee, milk, pumpkin puree and warming spices

Preparation time: 20 minutes

Cooking time: 5 minutes

Servings: 1 Cup

Ingredients

  • 1 shot of espresso or 60 ml strong coffee
  • 240 ml milk
  • 2 tablespoons pumpkin puree
  • 1–2 tablespoons sugar or maple syrup
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • A pinch of ground ginger
  • A pinch of ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)
  • Whipped cream (optional)

Method

  1. Brew one shot of espresso or prepare 60 ml of strong coffee.
  2. Combine the milk, pumpkin puree, sugar, cinnamon, ginger and nutmeg in a saucepan.
  3. Heat gently over medium-low heat, stirring continuously. Do not boil.
  4. Remove from the heat and add vanilla extract if desired.
  5. Froth the warm milk mixture using a milk frother or whisk.
  6. Pour the coffee into a large mug.
  7. Add the warm pumpkin-spiced milk and stir gently.
  8. Top with whipped cream and cinnamon if desired.

Pumpkin Pie

A traditional pumpkin pie is a delicious autumn dessert made with pumpkin puree, warm spices and a sweet pastry crust.

Preparation time: 20 minutes

Cooking time: 50–60 minutes

Servings: 8 slices

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked pie crust
  • 425 g pumpkin puree
  • 150 g sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 240 ml evaporated milk
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
  2. Place the pie crust in a pie dish.
  3. Mix the pumpkin puree, sugar and eggs in a large bowl until smooth.
  4. Add the evaporated milk, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg and salt. Mix well.
  5. Pour the filling into the pie crust.
  6. Bake for 15 minutes at 220°C.
  7. Reduce the temperature to 175°C (350°F) and bake for another 35–45 minutes.
  8. Allow the pie to cool before slicing.
