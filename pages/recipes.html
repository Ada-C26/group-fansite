Shakshuka

Eggs gently cooked in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce. Ready in 30 minutes and serves 2–3.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 4 eggs
  • Salt, pepper, and fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a pan and cook the onion and pepper until soft.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin, and paprika and stir for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Make 4 small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each one.
  5. Cover and cook until the egg whites are set, about 5–7 minutes.
  6. Top with parsley and serve with bread.
